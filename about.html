About us

Food Haven started with one idea: a good meal should not be complicated. We keep our menu short so we can cook every dish properly.

What we care about

We buy fresh vegetables and meat every morning. Our sauces and breads are made in our own kitchen. If something is not fresh, it does not go on a plate.

Our team

Our cooks and servers have worked together for years. If you have a favourite dish or an allergy we should know about, just tell us when you order.
